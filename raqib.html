MADARA SPEECH

“Tum shanti ki baat karte ho… lekin tumhari duniya ne sirf nafrat ko janam diya hai. Jahan bhi dekho, log apne sapnon ke liye doosron ke sapne tod dete hain. Har jeet ke peeche kisi ki haar hoti hai, aur har shanti ke peeche ek naya yuddh chhupa hota hai. Tum is duniya ko badalna chahte ho? Toh pehle iske sach ko samajhna hoga. Main hero banne nahi aaya… main tumhe woh sach dikhane aaya hoon jisse tum sab aankhen churaate rahe ho. Yaad rakhna—kabhi-kabhi sabse bada dushman woh nahi hota jo tumhare saamne khada hai… balki woh duniya hoti hai jise tum bachane ki koshish kar rahe ho.”
