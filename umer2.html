Umer shahzad

"Cricket ki duniya mein kai khiladi aaye, kai chale gaye. Lekin kuch naam aise hote hain jo sirf records nahi banate, balki itihaas likhte hain. Ek aisa hi naam hai — Virat Kohli. Ek ladka, jisne Delhi ki galiyon se apna safar shuru kiya aur duniya ke cricket stadiums mein apni pehchaan banayi. Yeh kahani hai talent ki, mehnat ki, haar se ladne ki aur kabhi haar na maanne ke jazbe ki."
